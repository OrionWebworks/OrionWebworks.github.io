Our Story

A Lifetime of Dedication

Mr Chow began his journey in the kitchens of Tsukiji at sixteen. Twenty years later, he brought his mastery to Singapore, opening a restaurant that honors tradition while embracing innovation.

Every morning, Chef Chow personally selects the finest fish from trusted suppliers. His philosophy is simple: respect the ingredient, honor the craft, delight the guest.

Chow has become a sanctuary for those who appreciate the subtle art of Japanese cuisine. Each visit is an invitation to slow down, savor, and discover.
