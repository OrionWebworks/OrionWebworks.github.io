The Visionary

MEET CHEN WEI LUN

Chen Wei Lun began his journey behind the camera over a decade ago, cutting his teeth on independent films before transitioning to commercial production and eventually founding CWL Film Academy in Singapore.

His philosophy is simple: great filmmaking can be taught, but it requires more than technical knowledge. It demands mentorship, hands-on experience, and an environment that nurtures creative courage.

Today, CWL Film Academy stands as Southeast Asia's most sought-after filmmaking institution, with alumni working across major studios, streaming platforms, and advertising agencies worldwide.
