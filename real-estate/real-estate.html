About

Design is not just what it looks like. Design is how it makes you feel.

With over 12 years of experience transforming spaces across Singapore and Southeast Asia, I believe every room has a story waiting to be told. My approach blends Eastern philosophies of harmony with contemporary Western design principles.

Each project begins with listening — understanding not just how you live, but how you want to feel in your space. From initial concept to final styling, I guide you through a collaborative journey that respects both your vision and the environment.
